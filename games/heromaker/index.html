Story & Objective

A mysterious girl you met in the village has vanished—captured by the forces of Malgor, Lord of Fear. Battle through 10 perilous lands (from the Village and Forest to the Volcano and Malgor's Lair) to rescue her!

How to Play, Wardrobe Refit & Cloud Exploration

  • Minion Shield Mechanic: Each land's Boss is protected by a cyan invulnerability shield until all minions in that stage are defeated.
  • Jump into the Clouds: Climb the platforms high into the sky cloud islands to find hidden Treasure Chests, Health Potions, and temporary Power-Up Orbs (2× Attack Fury, Swift Speed, and Aegis Shield)!
  • Hero Maker Wardrobe & Refit: Open the Hero Maker Wardrobe & Armory anytime (E key or HUD button) to inspect your Hero's 5 equipment slots around the live preview, see individual stat contributions & derived totals, click any gear card to buy/equip, or forge custom gear!
