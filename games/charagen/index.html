CharaGen Help Manual

Skeleton Builder

The core of your character. Use the tools to create a bone hierarchy.

  • Add Joint (J): Click anywhere to add a new joint.
  • Connect (C): Click a parent joint, then click a child joint to link them. The hierarchy is important for animation!
  • Select/Move (V): Drag joints to reposition them. If they are connected, moving a parent moves its children (Forward Kinematics), while moving a child attempts to drag the parent along (Inverse Kinematics).

Skin Editor

Flesh out your stick figure.

  • Toggle Enable Skin Layer to generate dynamic flesh around the bones.
  • Body Shape Preset: Quickly assign standard thicknesses to your character.
  • Select a joint, and in the Properties panel (right), adjust the Flesh Radius to make specific parts thicker or thinner.

Accessory Designer

Equip your character with custom items stored in your global library.

  • Global Library: Any item you draw or import is saved to your browser locally.
  • Click the + icon to draw a new item, or use the Import Image button in the header to upload a PNG/SVG.
  • Set Pivot: When drawing an item, click "Set Pivot" to define where the item attaches to the character (e.g., the hilt of a sword).
  • Anchors: To attach an item, select a bone connection, and click the + Anchor button in the Properties panel. Then click an item in your library to equip it to that anchor!

Animator & Timeline

Bring your character to life.

  • Select an animation profile like Walk or Attack from the bottom panel.
  • Move the playhead scrubber on the timeline. Pose your character, then click the Add Keyframe (K) button (the golden key) to save the pose at that frame.
  • The engine will automatically interpolate (tween) the movement between your keyframes.
  • Click Preview in the top left to see your animation isolated, and click Export as GIF to download a transparent game asset!
