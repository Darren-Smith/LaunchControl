Objective & Goblin Mine Mechanics

Venture into the flickering lantern-lit Goblin Mines! Both Miner 1 and Miner 2 carry heavy pickaxes/drills that can shatter diamond-bearing rocks and bash invading Rock Goblins.

  • Ore & Minecarts: Standard slate rocks yield 1 💎, rich Gold-Vein boulders yield 4 💎, and Crystal Clusters near the rail carts yield 6 💎.
  • Upgrades: Unlock shared upgrades at 50 💎 (Drill I), 100 💎 (Drill II), 150 💎 (Clockwork Auto-Bot), 300 💎 (Plasma Drill III), and 500 💎 (Minecart Overdrive).
  • Buddy Revive: If a miner hits 0 HP, they drop to one knee for 5 seconds (or stand up twice as fast if their buddy stands nearby). The run only ends if both miners are downed at once!
  • Victory: Defeat the Goblin Stone Colossus (spawns at 200 💎) AND fill the minecart quota of 1,000 💎!
