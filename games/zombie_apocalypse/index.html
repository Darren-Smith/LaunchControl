Story & Premise

The nefarious Zombie King has unleashed a glowing undead plague across Leo's hometown! Armed with his trusty blaster, flashlight, and grenades, Leo must fight through the City Streets, cross the foggy Countryside, and storm the Castle Interior to end the apocalypse.

How to Play & Scoring

  • Escape the Zone: Search the map and collect all 4 Golden Keys (🔑) (+100 Score each) to unlock the Escape Gate on the eastern border. Clearing all zombies in a zone reveals any remaining keys on the minimap!
  • Coins & Grenades: Every zombie slain drops a Gold Coin (+1 Score). Every 50 Coins automatically grants +1 Grenade (up to 10).
  • Smash & Loot: Shoot crates, bins, cars, and chests to uncover temporary power-ups: 🔫 Mega Bullets 🛡️ Shield (90% DR) ⚡ Double Shot 🧲 Coin Magnet ❤️ Extra Heart
