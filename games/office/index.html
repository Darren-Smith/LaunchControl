📘 School Office Security Handbook

Welcome to The Office! Check 3 things for every teacher in the queue before letting them through the School Office door:

  • 1. Banned / Allowed Watchlist: Roughly once a week, someone on the BANNED list queues up! Even if their expiry date is valid and their photo matches, if the Computer says 🚨 BANNED — DO NOT ADMIT, you must DENY them!
  • 2. Expiry Date Check: Check if the ID is expired! Both the Computer and the ID Card show the expiry date. If an ID is EXPIRED (before Oct 2026), DENY them!
  • 3. Visual Photo Match: Compare the Computer Portrait and the ID Badge Portrait carefully! Watch for different coloured ties, different coloured eyes, false teeth, pirate eye patches, different hair/wigs, fake moustaches, wrong glasses, or hats.
  • Happiness (105 Max) & Coffee (+8): Letting a dodgy/expired/banned teacher in costs -10 Happiness. Drink Coffee (2 cups/day, +8 Happiness each) by clicking the Coffee Machine in the office or pressing C!
