Version v1.3.0 Arcade Meadow Defense

🐝 Story & Objective

You are the guardian bee of the meadow's central hive! Harvest fresh flower nectar and golden airborne pollen to fill the hive's honeycomb cells and brew jars of golden honey—while stinging sneaky Burglar hands and pesticide-spraying Farmers before they ruin the harvest.

🌸 Mechanics & Scoring

🌸

Flowers (+5)

Fly close to blooming flowers to collect nectar drops (carry up to 3).

🌼

Good Pollen (+10)

Touch golden pollen in the air to carry it (up to 3). Deliver to the hive!

🟢

Bad Pollen (-20)

Avoid green pesticide pollen — it damages your health and resets combos!

🍯

Hive Brewing

Deposit nectar & pollen at the central hive. 3 Nectar + 3 Pollen brews 1 Honey Jar in 45s!

🦹

Burglar (+50)

Striped-sleeve hand swoops in to steal nectar (-100). Sting twice fast to repel!

👨‍🌾

Farmer (+30)

Checked-shirt hand sprays green pesticide on pollen & flowers. Sting to stop!

⚡

Sting Combos

Consecutive enemy stings build a 1.5× score multiplier per combo tier!

🏆

Victory Goal

Brew target honey jars to win — Easy: 5 jars · Normal: 10 jars · Hard: 15 jars.
