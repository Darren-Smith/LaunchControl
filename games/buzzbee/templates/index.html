📖 How to Play

⬆⬇⬅➡

Move

Arrow Keys or WASD to fly your bee around the screen

Space / Z

Sting

Press Space or Z to sting enemies when close. Costs stamina!

🌸

Flowers

Fly close to flowers to collect nectar (max 3 at a time)

🌼

Good Pollen

Touch yellow pollen to carry it (max 3). Deliver to hive!

🟢

Bad Pollen

Avoid green pesticide pollen — it damages you!

🍯

Hive

Fly to the hive (right side) to deposit nectar & pollen. 3+3 fills a hex, then brews honey in 45s!

🦹

Burglar

Striped-sleeve hand swoops down. Sting fast to stop theft! (+50)

👨‍🌾

Farmer

Check-shirt hand sprays pesticide, turns pollen green & kills flower nectar. Sting to stop! (+30)

⚡

Combo

Consecutive stings multiply your score for big bonuses!

🏆

Victory

Easy: 5 honey jars · Normal: 10 · Hard: 15
